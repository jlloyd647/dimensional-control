# 03 — Portal display and calibration

## Visual states
- **CLOSED**: subtle continuous dim cyan/blue scientific membrane; never visibly loops; no creature.
- **OPENING**: brightening/rippling transition to active media.
- **ACTIVE**: creature clip, no native controls or text.
- **UNSTABLE**: interruptible red pulses/flicker/interference; avoid intense strobing.
- **SEALING**: short return to closed membrane.

## Presentation
- Full viewport, no scrollbars or operator UI.
- Fit square 5×5-foot target without distorting 16:9 source; use contain/crop options.
- Use high-contrast imagery readable through construction plastic.
- Minimize CPU/GPU load; CSS effects preferred for first iteration.
- No browser chrome in fullscreen projection.

## Calibration
Operator-triggered alignment grid/test pattern; adjustable scale, X/Y offset, horizontal mirror for rear projection. Session-only persistence sufficient. Never show calibration controls to audience; projector may show test pattern during setup only.

## Playback
Transitions must be interruptible; a new action cannot leave multiple overlapping videos or stale animations. CLOSED is fallback on errors.
