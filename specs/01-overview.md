# 01 — Overview and scope

## Experience
The operator plays a scientist who has accidentally opened portals to other dimensions. Visitors help investigate and contain entities visible through a physical membrane. Encounters are selected and improvised, **not** a fixed looping show.

## Hardware
- One laptop with extended desktop and one projector.
- `/control` on laptop; `/portal` fullscreen on projector.
- Rear projection onto ~5 × 5 ft translucent construction sheeting, projector ~8 ft behind it.
- High-contrast creature silhouettes are primary media.

## Stack
Vite, React, TypeScript, CSS, BroadcastChannel, Web Audio API, local media assets. No backend, database, authentication, cloud service, or external runtime dependency.

## Principles
Reliable operation, fast iteration, operator-first controls, simple components, incremental delivery. Never implement future phases without request.

## MVP outcome
Operator can open/close portal, play an encounter, recover from errors, and fade layered ambient/tension audio while projected UI remains hidden.

## Deferred
DMX, fog/laser automation, physical buttons, AI dialogue, motion detection, mobile remotes, automatic scheduling, multi-projector support.
